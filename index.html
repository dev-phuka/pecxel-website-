Who we are

Engineering support with a builder's mindset.

pEc-XeL is a Malawian engineering and technology company focused on useful systems, dependable repairs, and community-centered innovation.

What makes pEc-XeL different

The company combines workshop repair discipline with electronics, electrical engineering, AI exploration, and practical training. That means clients can come for a phone repair, a wiring quote, a prototype, or a learning pathway and stay inside one technical ecosystem.

Our process

Clear steps. Careful work. Useful outcomes.

A straightforward approach keeps customers informed from the first conversation through testing and handover.

01 / Understand

Start with the real problem

We listen, inspect the device or site, and clarify what a successful result needs to look like.

02 / Plan

Agree on the next step

We explain suitable options, likely requirements, and a starting estimate before work moves ahead.

03 / Test

Check the work and hand over

We verify the repair or build and share clear guidance for using or maintaining the result.
