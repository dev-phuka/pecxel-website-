Our story

Engineering support with a builder's mindset.

pEcXeL is a Malawian engineering and technology company focused on useful systems, dependable repairs, and community-centered innovation.

A hands-on story, built around practical technology

Our story is rooted in a simple belief: technology should work for real people. From device repair and electrical work to electronics, automation, and practical training, pEcXeL brings different technical skills together to solve everyday challenges and develop ideas with purpose.

Our purpose

Build useful solutions

Provide innovative engineering services that improve everyday life and create opportunity.

Our vision

Grow African innovation

Become a trusted technology and engineering company with regional impact.
